Shareholder Governance, Rules & Regulations

1. General Governance & Framework

This portal outlines the rights, responsibilities, and operational procedures governing all equity holders of the company. Compliance with these terms is mandatory under the Corporate Charter and Bylaws.

2. Capital Structure & Share Adjustment Policy

Proportional Share Quantity Adjustments (Reverse Split / Consolidation):

  • Discretionary Adjustment: The company retains full authority to adjust or consolidate the total volume of outstanding shares at any time without prior individual member notice.
  • Equal Proportional Impact: Any total share count reduction (e.g., a 1% reduction in total share pool) automatically reduces each shareholder's individual unit count by the exact same percentage (1%).
  • Preservation of Equity Value: This adjustment strictly alters the numerical count of shares. Individual ownership percentage, relative voting power, and financial equity value remain 100% unaffected.
Example: If total shares decrease by 10%, a member holding 1,000 shares will now hold 900 shares. However, their total value and percentage share of the company remain identical.

3. Transfer of Shares & Pre-Emptive Rights

  • Right of First Refusal (ROFR): Shareholders proposing to sell, transfer, or assign equity must first offer those shares to existing shareholders on identical terms.
  • Lock-Up Period: Initial equity grants are subject to a standard restriction window prior to external transfer requests.
  • Board Approval: Any non-standard equity transfer requires explicit approval from the Board of Directors.

4. Voting, Meetings & Compliance

  • Annual General Meeting (AGM): Conducted annually with official notice provided to all voting shareholders.
  • Quorum: Corporate proceedings require representation of the designated minimum percentage of voting shares present in person or by proxy.
  • Confidentiality: Shareholders are bound by strict non-disclosure obligations regarding proprietary corporate metrics and strategic data.
